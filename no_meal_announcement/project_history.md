## 2026-05-30 — §8.1 weighting sensitivity investigated (LMM vs Method A divergence)

- Ran §8.1 locally off the analysis-ready CSV snapshot and noticed Table 8.1b (LMM, Method B) and Table 8.1a (Method A, per-user paired) **disagree in sign** on the stringent NMA arms (CE=0/BE=0, CE=0/BE≤1) for TIR/TAR/mean glucose; CE=0/BE≤∞ agrees.
- Root cause is a **weighting choice on identical per-user paired differences**, not a bug. A weighting decomposition (no subsampling) shows the random-intercept LMM coefficient equals the **harmonic effective-n (within-user precision) weighting** of the per-user diffs to ~0.06 across all 24 cells; **equal-user weighting = Method A**. Precision weighting upweights heavy-NMA-day users, who have NMA-favorable diffs (corr ≈ +0.10), flipping the sign. (Naive day-pooled OLS without the random intercept agrees with Method A, so it is the RE structure, not "day pooling".) Inclusion ruled out: refit on the 646 paired users only is unchanged.
- Driver: heavy-contributor concentration — top 5% of users hold ~69%/67% of CE=0/BE=0 / BE≤1 day-rows (median 2 days/user), vs 12% for CE>0.
- Conclusion: **CE=0/BE≤∞ robust** (NMA modestly better across all weightings); **stringent arms indeterminate** (sign-fragile, ~−3 to +3 TIR pts) — report no directional claim. CV robustly lower on NMA across all arms. Equal-user-weight (Method A) is the appropriate population characterization per §8.1/§11.
- Findings verified three independent ways (code review, statistical critique, from-scratch reproduction). Artifacts: [`docs/weighting_sensitivity.md`](docs/weighting_sensitivity.md), [`exploratory/lmm_weighting_sensitivity.py`](exploratory/lmm_weighting_sensitivity.py), `analysis/outputs/analysis_8_1/supplement/methodA_weighting_decomposition.csv` (relocated from `all/` 2026-05-31 — see that entry).

## 2026-06-01 — Autobolus reclassification: central bolus classifier wired into BE + delivery_strategy

Investigation (`exploratory/autobolus_as_normal_bolus.py`, `autobolus_hk_vs_dd_gap.sql`) found that Loop records autoboluses as `type='bolus'`, `subType='normal'` — indistinguishable from manual boluses by subType (~43% of all Loop boluses are automatic, all 'normal'). Two consequences on the old pipeline: BE (`subType='normal'`) silently counted autoboluses, so CE=0 autobolus days were never BE=0 — the CE=0/BE=0 and CE=0/BE≤1 arms excluded all autobolus users; and `delivery_strategy` (dd-only, `dd_autobolus_count >= 3`) missed ~97% of HealthKit-tagged autobolus days, labeling ~2% of days `autobolus_on` when the true in-cohort rate is ~72%.

- **Detection signals:** HealthKit `MetadataKeyAutomaticallyIssued` flag (authoritative, but absent on ~50% of normal boluses — "HK-silent") + a dosingDecision fallback (loop-DD prior 5s, no `normalBolus` DD ±15s). §5 of the exploration sized the fallback: 3.56M (7.46%) of normal boluses are HK-silent + dd-automatic, recovered at only ~0.11% conflict with explicit HK-manual — so the fallback is warranted (HK alone is insufficient).
- **New staging script `export_user_day_bolus_classification.py`** → `nma_user_day_bolus_classification` (per user-day: manual / automatic counts + hk/dd breakdown). HK-first / dd-fallback rule; deduped on (user, nearest-min, units) with the automatic signal MAX-aggregated across duplicate (HealthKit + Loop-direct) representations. Single source of truth; closes the pipeline-wide blind spot — `subType='normal'` + HK-silent + dd-automatic boluses are counted by neither `dd_autobolus_count` (needs subType≠'normal') nor `hk_autobolus_count` (needs the flag).
- **Wired (NMA first; FDA later):** BE = `manual_normal_bolus_count` — `export_user_day_bolus_counts.py` now projects the classifier (no longer reads BDDP; supersedes the interim HK-only exclusion). `delivery_strategy` = `automatic_bolus_count >= 3` in `export_user_day_analysis_ready.py` (replaces the interim `GREATEST(dd,hk)`); analysis-ready now carries `automatic_bolus_count` / `auto_hk_count` / `auto_dd_count` instead of `dd_/hk_autobolus_count`. ## 2026-06-01 — §8.3 wired + finding-explanation supplement + weighting supplement retired

Structural reframe: CE=0 ⟺ `carb_grams_total = 0` exactly, so a CE=0 day is the zero endpoint of the announced-carb axis → leading explanation is an **intake confound**, not "disengaged dosing is better."
