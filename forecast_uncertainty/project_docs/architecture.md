# forecast_uncertainty — Architecture

## Current state (as of 2026-09-09)

**Where the project stands.** Prediction intervals for a deterministic glucose forecast, built and evaluated on the cohort-B behavior traces with real therapy settings, at 13 horizons (5 … 30 / 60 / 90 / 120 / 180 / 240 / 300 / 360 min). The interval is `predicted + location(state, h) + scale(state, h) × [q_lo, q_hi]_h`; both models share ONE structure (horizon-interacted features + hour terms) and one candidate set per forecaster: the forecaster's own FORECAST TERMS plus five shared STATE features (IOB × ISF, glucose level, prior 30-min change, recent carbs and bolus in mg/dL). Lower bound floored at 40 mg/dL. Evaluated by PIT / CRPS under temporal and leave-one-user-out splits, a nested-spec comparison with conditional coverage on ONE origin-state taxonomy (`model/origin_states.py`: intervention state × level × trend; adopted 2026-09-08), a model LADDER (figures 19–20), and an online adaptive level. The object of comparison is Loop's DISPLAYED forecast against persistence; the reconstructed forecasters now run on the full insulin history (controller basal and autoboluses installed 2026-09-08) and their ladder is being recomputed (`outputs_sweep/chain_ci.sh`).

**Findings that shape the next steps.** (1)–(6) The dose-magnitude terms (IOB × ISF, recent bolus) can leave the LOCATION at no cost (iteration cap raised: LOUO 16.86 vs 16.96 for Loop's forecast, nine of the held-out users better; coverage unchanged); the location's coefficient on Loop's predicted change is −0.53 at 5 min and about −0.8 beyond an hour, holding level and IOB fixed. (7) A hinge on the predicted rise closes the predicted-rise under-coverage (0.91 → 0.95) at no CRPS cost — default-spec decision pending; post-bolus-no-carb origins remain the one under-covered state (0.92–0.93). Not FDA-scoped. Dates and numbers in project_history.md.

**Operational rule:** the residual tables are several GB in memory; fold workers load only `evaluation_columns(out_dir)`, cap training rows at 400k, and run two at a time — a six-worker sweep rebooted the machine on 2026-09-06. `run_residuals.py` alone peaks near 20 GB (`--fit-only` resumes from a written table). **Next:** read the chain (ladder on the rebuilt full-Loop table with `location_no_insulin_channel`; figure 20 across three forecasters; figures 21–23 and Level A on the states); then, in the user's order, the systematic intervention-response analysis, the titration next cuts (a lower level or per-user interval, the gate judged with the automatic dosing, noisy sensor, more physiologies, the path-wise level, the descriptive dose sweep at real meals), the predicted-rise under-coverage; the per-user layer stays parked.

**Scope (user, 2026-09-08):** the forecaster of interest is Loop's own displayed forecast (`loop_displayed`, series and bolus-time decisions, and the meal channel). Persistence stays as the reference line in the point-forecast figures; the reconstructed forecasters (`loop_static`, `loop_full`) and their ladders are diagnostics, run only when asked.

**The interval as the application factor (2026-09-08/09):** the simulator controller can replace Loop's fixed 0.4 with the largest share of the correction whose calibrated floor holds (`titration/floor_gate.py`, `autobolus_policy`); on one physiology it delivers 15–29% more automatic insulin with 3–6 more minutes in range per six hours and lows unchanged, while the full correction every cycle is catastrophic; on real decisions it holds back at the few percent of decisions carrying three to four times the hypoglycaemia rate. The population grid (three physiologies × ideal / noisy sensor × unannounced intake) is the current run (`outputs/chains/chain_population.sh`).

**Memory rule for the evaluation scripts.** Measure one fold worker before choosing `--jobs` (`/usr/bin/time -l` → maximum resident set size; 6.1 GB per worker on 2026-09-06), keep jobs × peak under ~40% of RAM (two workers on the 36 GB laptop), keep `--max-train-rows 400000`, cap BLAS threads at 2 per worker, run stages strictly one after another (`outputs/chains/chain_displayed.sh` is the template), and run nothing else heavy alongside.

## Data

Cohort-B traces: `behavior_model/data/behavior_traces_b/{users,cgm,carbs,boluses,dosing}.csv` (pseudonymized ids; continuous per-user windows; 5-min tick contract from behavior_model §6). Known limits: no basal / autobolus / settings columns; `cob` all-NaN until the pending COB re-export; Loop forecasts not exported (re-export with `bgForecast` is the drop-in path).

## Caveats carried into every result
- Residuals are against realized glucose under closed-loop operation (controller intervention is inside the residual; autoboluses are not in the export, so it cannot be corrected).
- Insulin history = user-initiated boluses only; scheduled basal treated as neutral (Loop convention). The controller's temp-basal deviations and autoboluses are NOT in the forecast (median +0.4 U of a typical 1.3 U displayed IOB; −0.9 to +3.0 U across origins) and are strongly glucose-dependent, so the static forecast is biased low when glucose is low and high when it is high for reasons unrelated to its curves. Fix pending: export the basal and automated-bolus streams.
- Carb absorption time is Loop's 180-min default for every entry: the export's `absorption_minutes` column is empty.
- Timezone: one per-user offset (`tz_offset_min`), applied once in `run_residuals.py`; the stream-timing QC figure (meals peak at local breakfast / lunch) confirms it.
