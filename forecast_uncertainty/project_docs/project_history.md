## 2026-09-08 — one set of origin states for the whole project (PROPOSED, awaiting approval)
- Prompted by figure 20: persistence rows show three coverage states and Loop rows five, because "predicted rise" and "predicted fall" are defined by the sign of the forecaster's own predicted change, undefined for persistence. The project defines origin states five different ways (post-meal / other and predicted rise / fall in the ladder's conditional coverage; three overlapping 60-min event windows in figures 21–22; three disjoint 60-min sets in figure 23; meal vs correction bolus by other windows in figure 22; insulin-recommended vs not in figure 26).
- Proposal: one module defines the states; `residuals.py` writes an `origin_state` column into every table; every consumer reads it. Principles: known at the origin (no forecast, no outcome); exclusive and exhaustive per axis; anchored on the measured drivers of error; windows from the model's own clock features and Loop's physiology. PRIMARY axis, intervention state: post-carb early (0–60 min since a carb entry), post-carb late (60–180), post-bolus no carb (bolus within 180 min, no entry within 180), quiet (neither within 180). 60 min = where Loop's forecast beats persistence and the spread steps up; 180 = Loop's default absorption time, the meal-clock cap, the clean-event gap, and where the spread returned to baseline in figure 24. SECONDARY axes: glucose level at the origin (< 70 / 70–180 / > 180, the consensus time-in-range bands); trend from the prior 30-min change (falling / flat / rising at 1 mg/dL per minute, the CGM trend-arrow convention, replacing the ad hoc 15 mg/dL split in earlier entries). Predicted rise / fall survive only as an optional forecaster-dependent "forecast direction" diagnostic. Figures 20–23 and 26 move to the taxonomy; figure 22's meal / correction boluses become post-carb early / post-bolus no carb at bolus-time origins; the clean event of figures 24–25 stays as a subset of post-carb early. The fitted model does not change. Open judgment call: 180 vs 60 min for the post-bolus window (180 proposed so the group is large enough to report coverage on).
